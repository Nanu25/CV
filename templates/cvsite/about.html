My story

I'm in my third year at Babeș-Bolyai University in Cluj-Napoca, pursuing my passion for computer science. From a young age I was curious about how technology works, and programming became the key to understanding and shaping that world.

My interest took off in high school with algorithms in C++. By 10th grade I had mastered the school curriculum and joined the Center for Performance in Informatics, where I dove into advanced algorithms and data structures. In my final year I had the chance to teach younger students advanced algorithmic techniques.

I also completed Harvard's CS50 course, which introduced me to new technologies and gave me the confidence to take on more complex projects.

You can explore my work in the projects section.
